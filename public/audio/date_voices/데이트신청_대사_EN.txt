데이트 신청 대사 (9명, 각 3문장) - EN
==========================================
1. 미야자와 리나 (승무원)
  1. Um... on your day off, if it's alright with you, would you like to spend some time together?
  2. If you have the time, I'd like you to go on a date with me.
  3. On your next day off, I wish I could spend it with you... that's what I was hoping.
2. 타치바나 미사키 (요가강사)
  1. Hey, boss, want to go hang out somewhere together next time?
  2. Next day off, want to go somewhere just the two of us?
  3. Won't you go on a date with me? I'm sure it'll be fun!
3. 사토 메구미 (간호사)
  1. Um... if it's alright with you, would you... go out with me...?
  2. If it's alright... could you spare me some time...?
  3. I want to go somewhere with you... that's how I feel...
4. 아키야마 미호 (여경)
  1. ...If you're free, how about we get together.
  2. This weekend, let's go out just the two of us.
  3. ...Sometimes, I want to go out with you.
5. 센노 리나 (발레리나)
  1. When you have the time, would you go out with me?
  2. If it's alright with you, please give me your next day off.
  3. I've been thinking that I'd like to go on a date with you.
6. 사쿠라기 마이 (재즈싱어)
  1. Hey, want to go out just the two of us sometimes?
  2. Want to go grab a drink together? I'll pay.
  3. I figured spending time with you wouldn't be so bad, you know.
7. 루이자 (삼바댄서)
  1. Boss, want to go hang out together?
  2. Let's go on a date next time! When works for you?
  3. I want to do something fun together, just us two~! What do you say?
8. 리메이 (중식당 사장)
  1. When you're free, how about we grab a meal sometime.
  2. Next day off, won't you go out with me?
  3. Sometimes, I want to take it slow with you.
9. 시라카와 아야 (교수)
  1. ...If it's alright, how about tea with me?
  2. If it's not a bother, how about dinner together.
  3. I'd like to spend a little time together with you, taking it slow.
